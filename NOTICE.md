## Public transit data

The bundled catalog is derived from the Nederlandse GTFS feed retrieved from https://gtfs.ovapi.nl/nl/gtfs-nl.zip on 2026-11-12 (source publication 2026-11-11). NDOV open data are distributed as CC0; see https://govi.nu/ .

Bundled operator fleet records are derived from the publicly available CXX, EBS and Qbuzz NeTEx vehicle exports through https://data.ndovloket.nl/netex/ . Each export URL is retained in assets/fleet.js. Public vehicle registration make/model metadata was joined via the RDW open-data service, dataset m9d7-ebf2. No registration-holder data are included.

Transitous and the sources it aggregates have their own licenses. Service attribution and links to https://transitous.org/sources/ are displayed in the application. Follow the open-source, noncommercial, low-resource usage policy at https://transitous.org/api/ .
